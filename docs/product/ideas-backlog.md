# Ideas Backlog

Not scheduled. Ordered roughly by value to campus vendors and students, then effort. Verify demand in the pilot before building.

## Trust and safety (build early; they protect the brand)
- **Reviews only from verified purchasers or attendees** (no fake reviews), with a vendor reply.
- **Report a vendor/listing** and a simple dispute flow for manual-transfer orders.
- **Response-time badge** ("usually replies within 10 min") from real chat data.
- **Scam warning copy** at manual-transfer checkout (confirm in your bank app; never trust a screenshot).

## Campus-specific
- **Open/closed now** toggle and opening hours on every vendor.
- **Pre-order batches with a cut-off** ("order by Thursday 6pm for Saturday's bake"), which fits perishables and cakes.
- **Hostel/zone delivery areas** with simple per-zone fees.
- **Semester calendar mode:** exam-week promos, resumption-week welcome offers, graduation season for photographers and event planners.
- **Student group orders** (split a catering order, group-buy discounts).
- **Event add-ons:** QR check-in app mode, guest list import, organiser team roles.
- **Ambassadors per hostel/faculty** with referral credit (extra free weeks).

## Low-data and low-network reality
- **Light mode:** compressed images, no autoplay, text-first listings.
- **Offline-capable vendor dashboard** (PWA): see orders and confirm transfers with weak signal, sync later.
- **Share cards:** one tap generates a clean image/link of a product or page for WhatsApp status and Instagram.
- **Local language options** (e.g. Pidgin, Yoruba, Hausa, Igbo) for key screens and notifications.

## Vendor growth tools
- **Order-again links and repeat-customer list** with a gentle "message your regulars" prompt.
- **Simple promo codes and bundle prices**; low-stock alerts; sold-out toggle in two taps.
- **Weekly summary** ("this week: 23 orders, 6 new customers, top item") by email or WhatsApp-ready text.
- **Quote requests with templates** (tailors, printers, planners), and deposit tracking.
- **Business-of-the-week** feature (earned by ratings and reliability, not paid placement).

## Customer delight
- **Saved vendors, price-drop and restock alerts, event reminders.**
- **Order/booking timeline** with clear status and a "how it works" explainer for first-time manual-transfer buyers.

## Platform and revenue (later)
- Custom websites on the same backend; custom domains; branded receipts and invoices.
- Annual plans, a founding-member price lock, and a Verified badge programme.
- Delivery partner integrations; USSD/SMS fallback; loyalty points.
