# Pilot Campus Plan (answers "item 6")

**What this question means:** the platform works best when many businesses and buyers are in one place. So the launch is not "all of Nigeria". It is one campus (plus the area around it) with 20-50 real businesses on Fluo.

## Choose the campus by
1. You can physically be there, or have a trusted student rep there.
2. A large, dense student population living on or near campus.
3. Many existing vendors (food, phones, beauty, barbers, events).
4. Active WhatsApp vendor groups (proof of the problem you described).

## Recruit the first 20-50 businesses
- Aim for a spread: food, phones/devices, beauty/barbers, a few event organisers, 2-3 service people.
- Set each one up in person (about 10 minutes), put their Fluo link in their WhatsApp status, and add their payout account.
- Offer the free tier; ask for one honest weekly feedback message.

## Measure (weekly)
Businesses live, listings, link shares, orders/bookings/tickets, repeat buyers, and what vendors ask for that is missing.

## Needed from you
Which campus (or area), and a rough list of businesses you already know there. Then we can fill in the first-campus seed list.
