# Fluo — Complete Marketing Playbook, Campaign Strategy & Generation Prompts

This handbook provides the end-to-end go-to-market strategy, brand positioning, audience segmentation, copy scripts, and generative AI prompts (for Midjourney, DALL-E, Runway Gen-3, and Sora) to produce high-converting flyers, social banners, billboard ads, and viral short-form video commercials across all Fluo verticals and capabilities.

## Part 1: General Brand Strategy & Positioning

### Brand Essence:
Fluo is **"The Operating System for Campus & Community Commerce."**
It turns any smartphone into a digital storefront, an offline-capable POS counter, a secure escrow handoff terminal, and a ticket gate pass.

### Core Value Propositions:
1. **For Merchants & Shop Owners**: "Sell online, sell at the counter, stop writing receipts by hand, and eliminate fake bank transfer fraud."
2. **For Customers & Students**: "Order food, split the bill with friends, ride keke without price gouging, buy verified event tickets, and never get scammed on deliveries."
3. **For Riders & Couriers**: "Get fair fares through open price bidding, avoid getting stranded, and get paid instantly upon PIN release."

### Elevator Pitches:
- **30-Second Student Pitch**: *"Ever tried splitting a ₦15,000 shawarma order with four roommates and spent two hours chasing everyone for their share? Or bought sneakers on Instagram and prayed they actually deliver? Fluo lets you pool money in a shared cart with friends so everyone pays their own share directly, and protects your deliveries with a 4-digit escrow PIN that releases money only when you inspect the shoes."*
- **30-Second Merchant Pitch**: *"Tired of POS machines showing network error while customer transfers are delayed? Fluo gives you a high-speed touch POS register right on your phone or tablet, calculates exact cash change, prints branded receipts with your store name, and tracks your daily profits automatically with zero paperwork."*

## Part 2: Feature-Specific Campaigns & Angle Matrix

| Feature | Primary Pain Point | Core Campaign Angle | Target Audience |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Escrow Delivery Hand-Off** | Online shoppers getting scammed; fake payment alerts sent to sellers. | *"Inspect Before You Release"* — Your money stays locked until you give the rider your 4-digit PIN. | Online shoppers, boutique buyers, tech buyers. |
| **Group Cart / Split Payment** | One person getting stuck with the whole lunch bill while friends 'forget' to transfer. | *"No More Debt Reminders"* — Add your meal, pay your share, food is on the way. | Students, roommates, office lunch groups. |
| **POS Kiosk Mode** | Slow manual checkout, handwriting paper receipts, miscalculated change. | *"Your Phone is Now a Supermarket POS"* — Tap items, enter cash, calculate change, print receipts. | Campus tuck shops, supermarkets, canteens. |
| **Mobility Bidding** | Keke and okada drivers overcharging in rain or traffic; riders stranded late. | *"Name Your Price, Find Your Ride"* — Propose your fare, negotiate fairly, ride safely. | Campus commuters, dispatch parcel senders. |
| **Gate QR Ticketing** | Counterfeit ticket screenshots, gate fights, overcrowding, offline network outage. | *"Scan In in 1 Second"* — Cryptographically signed QR passes that work 100% offline. | Club owners, event planners, student unions. |
