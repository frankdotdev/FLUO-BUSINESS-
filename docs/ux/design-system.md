# Fluo Design System (draft)

**Status:** in progress. Tokens are in `design-tokens.css`.

## References, used as inspiration only
- Marketplace reference: product-card density, shop/cart/profile structure. **Not adopted:** gradients, video/live-stream, coins/gifting, glass effects.
- Geometric reference: bold type, disciplined black/white/one-green palette, flat shapes. **Adopted idea:** single solid accent, strong typographic hierarchy, flat geometric brand marks.

## Rules
No gradients, glassmorphism or decorative emoji. One accent. One icon family (Lucide). Image-led cards with reserved aspect ratios. Dashboards are task-first (tables, lists, calendars), not oversized metric-card grids. Empty states point to the first useful action; no fake metrics.

## Typeface
Inter placeholder; final choice open.

## Mobile navigation (customer)
Home · Explore · Orders · Inbox · Account, plus a workspace switcher (Personal / each business with role).
